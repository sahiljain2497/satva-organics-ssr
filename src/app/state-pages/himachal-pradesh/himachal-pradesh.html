Why Himachal Farmers Choose Satva

Himachal’s acidic, shallow mountain soils often lock up nutrients even when chemical fertilizers are applied. Satva’s vermicompost—enriched with neem, mustard cake, and beneficial microbes including Trichoderma—helps buffer soil pH, improve water retention on slopes, and unlock nutrients already present in the soil.

  • Apple orchards: 5–8 kg per mature tree during pre-monsoon prep (April–May)
  • Stone fruits & pears: Improved fruit size, colour, and mandi rates
  • Vegetables: Better root development in polyhouses and open fields

Local Context

With over 1 lakh hectares under apple cultivation, Himachal is India’s apple heartland. Progressive growers in Shimla district and beyond are switching to organic inputs for premium produce and long-term soil health.
